Reunion Route – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026 · Applies to the Reunion Route game for Android, version 1.0.0 and later.

Reunion Route ("the game") is made by Buffalo Casual Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: the game has no accounts, we do not collect or receive your personal information, and your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

1. Information stored only on your device

The game saves your progress (levels, stars, Cheer, hints, decorations), your settings (language, sound, music, vibration, high contrast, tutorial hand) and an unfinished puzzle layout in a file on your device. This file is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob): short ads you choose to watch for a reward, and a full-screen ad after some puzzle results. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect the following from your device:

InformationUsed for
IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate locationAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Device and advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID and app set ID)Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Interactions with ads (for example ad views and taps) and app launchesAdvertising, analytics
Diagnostic information (for example crash logs and performance data of the ads SDK)Analytics, keeping ads working

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us; it is sent encrypted. Google's use of information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google's own list for the Mobile Ads SDK: Google Play data disclosure.

3. Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): when you first open the game you may be asked, through Google's consent message, whether ads can be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings › Privacy choices.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete it, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Google settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads on most devices).
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the game deletes all data stored by the game on your device. Settings › Start over in the game deletes your progress.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game has no account, chat, or social features, and no in-app purchases in this version.
  • We do not sell personal information for money. Some US state laws may treat ads personalization through third parties as "sharing" or "targeted advertising"; you can opt out with the choices in section 3.

5. Children

The game is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.
